# ArmDemo Engineering Log — 2026.9.5

## English

### Goals

- Close the two remaining no-hardware software gaps from the team's "Arm 与 Elevator 开发" plan: settable max speed / max acceleration limits, and a third preset position per mechanism with per-action completion timing.
- Keep the through-bore encoder switch as a pure placeholder — no changes until the hardware arrives.

### Completed Work

- Added the spec's speed and acceleration ceilings without changing the validated open-loop voltage motion: movement voltages now ramp at most a configurable volts-per-second, and cut to zero when the measured motion already runs at the configured cap in the commanded direction. The arm uses its filtered angle velocity; the elevator gained finite-difference extension velocities normalized by each motor's own travel so the two mechanisms (3.4 vs 14.5 rotations) stay coordinated. All four ceilings are live-tunable on NetworkTables (`Arm Max Speed (deg/s)`, `Arm Voltage Slew (V/s)`, `Elevator Max Travel (/s)`, `Elevator Voltage Slew (V/s)`); `0` disables a limit. Defaults (60°/s, 60 V/s, 1.5 travels/s) are intentionally generous so the on-robot feel is unchanged until the tuning weeks tighten them.
- Hold outputs are deliberately not ramped (they are already gentle by design and the stop must stay instant), and the ramp reference resets on stop and on hold latch so every movement starts from zero.
- Added the third preset to each mechanism: button 5 moves the arm to the middle working angle (default 45°, ±1.5° window) from whichever side it is on — the approach keeps the 0.8V floor in both directions because mid-travel is not a gravity-settling rest; button 6 moves the elevator to half of each motor's own travel (1.7 / 7.25 rotations), extending from below or retracting from above, with the same "whichever motor arrives first stops both" rule as the hard limits and the same arm interlock as the other elevator buttons.
- Every movement command (arm up/down/middle, elevator extend/retract/middle) now measures its own runtime: the duration is printed to the robot console and published as `LastMoveDurationSeconds` / `LastMoveActionName` telemetry — the data for the spec's per-action completion-time records. Commands were rebuilt on `FunctionalCommand` so the finish/interrupt path records the same way.
- Fixed two of my own first-draft bugs caught by the new tests: the slew helper returned the target instead of the current voltage when the timestep was zero, and the arm mid command finished instantly when started from the top because the finish condition treated "already at the upper limit" as a stop even though the travel direction was away from it (the condition now only watches the wall that is actually on the path).
- 12 new tests covering the slew/speed helpers, the mid commands in both directions on both mechanisms, the descending-to-mid approach floor, and duration/name recording; 45 tests green, full build passes.

### Next Steps

- On the next robot session: verify buttons 5/6 map to the intended physical buttons (expected left/right bumper on this controller's Xbox-style raw IDs), and watch `LastMoveDurationSeconds` for the first real timing records.
- Through-bore encoders: purchase, then fill in the placeholder constants — untouched in this change.
- Notion page updated the same day with the status column, progress section, and tracker row.

## 中文

### 今日目标

- 补齐《Arm 与 Elevator 开发》计划中剩余的两项无硬件软件工作：可设置的最大速度/加速度限制，以及每套机构的第三个预设位与动作完成计时。
- Through-bore 编码器切换保持纯占位——硬件到货前不做任何改动。

### 已完成工作

- 在不改变已验证开环电压运动的前提下加入规格要求的速度/加速度上限：运动电压每秒最多变化可配置的伏特数（加速度限制）；当测量到的运动在指令方向上已达上限时运动电压立即归零（速度限制）。机械臂复用滤波角速度；Elevator 新增按各自行程归一化的差分伸出速度（3.4 圈与 14.5 圈两机构由此保持协同）。四项上限全部可在 NetworkTables 在线调节（`Arm Max Speed (deg/s)`、`Arm Voltage Slew (V/s)`、`Elevator Max Travel (/s)`、`Elevator Voltage Slew (V/s)`）；设 `0` 关闭。默认值（60°/s、60 V/s、1.5 行程/s）刻意放宽，真机手感在调参周收紧之前不变。
- 保持命令的输出刻意不做斜坡（本身变化柔和，且停止必须即时）；斜坡基准在 stop 与保持锁存时归零，每个动作都从零电压起步。
- 两套机构各增加第三个预设位：按键 5 把机械臂从任意一侧移到中间工作角（默认 45°，±1.5° 窗口）——接近段双向保留 0.8V 底压，因为中段不是重力自持的静息点；按键 6 把 Elevator 移到各电机自身行程的一半（1.7 / 7.25 圈），从下方伸出或从上方收回，沿用硬限位"任一电机先到即停"的规则，并与其他 Elevator 按键一样受机械臂联锁保护。
- 每个运动命令（机械臂升/降/中位、Elevator 伸/收/中位）现在测量自身运行时长：完成后打印到机器人控制台，并以 `LastMoveDurationSeconds` / `LastMoveActionName` 遥测量发布——即规格"每个动作均记录完成时间"的数据来源。命令改为基于 `FunctionalCommand` 构建，正常结束与被打断的记录路径一致。
- 修复新测试抓住的两个初稿 bug：斜坡助手在时间步为零时返回了目标值而不是当前值；机械臂中位命令从顶部启动时会立即结束——结束条件把"已在上限位"当作停止，即使行进方向是离开它（改为只关注真正位于路径上的那面墙）。
- 新增 12 个测试，覆盖斜坡/限速助手、两套机构双向中位命令、向中位下降的底压、时长与名称记录；45 个测试全绿，整包构建通过。
